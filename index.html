LOVE ME

A little question, from a very hopeful heart.

💖

Will you be my date?

Say yes... pretty please 🌹

💞

You said yes!

So this one is for you...

Under a sky of softly burning stars,

I found a light that carries your name.

Every road I walked, however far,

led me quietly back to the same flame.

Your laughter is my favorite melody,

your eyes, the harbor where my storms grow still.

If tonight were only a small mystery,

I'd spend it solving you, and always will.

So take my hand, let evening be our guide,

through candlelight and whispered, golden hours.

With you, my heart has nothing left to hide,

it blooms like roses, wild among the flowers.

Tonight, and every night, my answer's true:

I'm yours, my love. I'm glad you chose me too.

Forever yours 💋
